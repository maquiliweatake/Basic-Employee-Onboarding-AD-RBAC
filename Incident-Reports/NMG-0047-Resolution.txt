Incident NMG-0047 — Resolution Report

Issue
Jane Cooper did not have the access expected for her role in the Human Resources department.

Findings
Her account was placed in the wrong organizational unit (OU) and was missing membership in the HR-Users security group.

Actions Taken
Jane Cooper’s account was moved to OU=HR,DC=NMG,DC=com. Her membership in the HR-Users security group was corrected.

Verification
The account’s placement in the HR OU and its membership in HR-Users were verified and documented with screenshots.

Outcome
Both configuration issues identified during the investigation of incident NMG-0047 were corrected.
